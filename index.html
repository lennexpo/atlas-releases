Install in three steps

It takes about two minutes.

Download

Press the Download button above and wait for Atlas-Setup to finish.

Run the installer

Open the file. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, press More info, then Run anyway.

Set up and start

Enter your company details and email. We send you a 6-digit code. Type it in and Atlas is ready.

Why does Windows show that box?

Atlas is new and not yet signed with a paid Windows certificate, so Windows is careful with anything it has not seen before. The installer comes straight from Invitt Digital. Only download Atlas from this page.

Questions

Short answers to what people ask first.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your records are saved on your computer. Only the licence check talks to Invitt, and it sends no business records.

How do I get a new version?

Atlas tells you when a new version is ready. Press Download, run the new installer, and your records stay as they are. You can also check in Settings, under About.

Does it work on Mac or phone?

Not yet. This pilot is for Windows 10 and 11 only.
