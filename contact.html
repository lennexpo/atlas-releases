Good questions.
Straightforward answers.

What can I manage with Atlas?

Quotations, invoices, receipts, clients and your books, with ZIMRA dates and tax estimates alongside. Everything is saved on your own computer.

Can I see a returning client’s history?

Yes. Open a client and see their quotations, invoices, payments and what is still owed, all in one place.

Can I keep track of unpaid invoices?

Yes. Atlas shows who still owes you and lets you record each payment against its invoice.

Can my team use it too?

Atlas runs on one Windows computer for now. Team access is not part of this pilot.

How do I get started?

Download Atlas, run the installer, then enter your company details and the 6-digit code we email you. It takes about two minutes. See Install in three steps.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your records are saved on your computer. Only the licence check talks to Invitt, and it sends no business records.

How do I get a new version?

Atlas tells you when a new version is ready. Press Download, run the new installer, and your records stay as they are. You can also check in Settings, under About.

Does it work on Mac or phone?

Not yet. This pilot is for Windows 10 and 11 only.
