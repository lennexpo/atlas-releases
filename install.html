Install in three steps

It takes about two minutes.

Download

Press the Download button above and wait for Atlas-Setup to finish.

Run the installer

Open the file and follow the prompts until the installer finishes.

Set up and start

Enter your company details and email. We send you a 6-digit code. Type it in and Atlas is ready.

If something does not go to plan

My 6-digit code did not arrive.

Check the email address you typed, then look in your spam or junk folder. The message comes from atlas@mail.invitt.co.zw. A code works once and for 10 minutes. If it has expired, wait a minute and ask Atlas for a new one. Still nothing? Contact us and we will sort it out.

Do I need the internet to use Atlas?

You need it once, to get your code. After that Atlas keeps working without internet. It checks in quietly about once a week, and allows up to 14 days without a connection.

Can I install Atlas on another computer, or reinstall it?

Yes. Sign up with the same email and you get the same licence and the same end date, so there is no second trial. Your records stay on the computer where you made them, so back them up in Atlas before you move.

How do I update Atlas?

When a newer version is out, Atlas shows a card with a Download button. Download it, run the installer, and your records are kept.

Is there a printed guide?

Yes. The pilot guide (PDF) covers installing, your first 15 minutes, backups and who to contact.
