Questions about paying

Which email should I use?

Use the same email you sign in with in Atlas. Your licence is tied to that email, so it is how we know which account to extend.

How do I pay?

You are taken to a secure payment page and choose how to pay there. We never see your card or wallet details, and your receipt is emailed to you.

What currency are prices in?

US dollars.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Your records stay on your own computer. Atlas pauses until your licence is renewed, and your data is not deleted.

Can I talk to someone first?

Yes. WhatsApp us and we will help you choose.
