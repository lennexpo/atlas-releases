Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Invitt Digital (Private) Limited (“Invitt”, “we”, “us”) collects when you use Atlas and this website, and what we do with it.

The short version: your business records stay on your own computer. We do not receive your invoices, clients, accounts or bank details. We only hold the sign-up details needed to give you a licence.

1. What stays on your computer

Everything you enter into Atlas, including company details, clients, invoices, receipts, tasks, HR and accounting records, is stored on your own computer. Atlas has no cloud sync, and it does not send these records to us. Backups you make are saved on your computer too.

2. What we collect

  • Sign-up details. When you request your access code, you give us your name, company name, email address and phone number.
  • Licence information. Your licence records your email, name, company, status, start date and end date. Atlas contacts our licence service about once a week to renew it, and sends the licence to do so.
  • Technical information. Our licence service sees your internet (IP) address and the time of each request. We use this to prevent abuse, for example to limit how many codes can be requested.
  • Update checks. Atlas asks this website for the latest version number. This is an ordinary web request, so the site’s hosting may record your IP address. No business records are sent.
  • Messages you send us. If you email or WhatsApp us, we receive what you send and your contact details. Atlas has a Feedback button, but it only prepares a message. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your own email or WhatsApp. If you send it, the message includes your company name, signer name, phone, email, rating, your answers and the Atlas version.

This website does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

3. How we use it

  • to give you a licence and email you your access code;
  • to renew, extend, suspend or end licences, and to manage the limited pilot places and waiting list;
  • to keep the service secure and prevent abuse;
  • to answer your messages and improve Atlas;
  • to tell you about important changes to Atlas or your licence.

We do not sell your information.

4. Services that help us run Atlas

We use trusted providers to operate Atlas, and they may process your information on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare hosts our licence service and its database.
  • Resend sends the access code emails.
  • GitHub hosts the Atlas installer file that you download.

These providers may process information in countries outside Zimbabwe. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up and licence details while your licence is active and for a reasonable time afterwards, for example to avoid giving a second trial to the same email address. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email us and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can. Deleting your licence details means we can no longer renew your licence.

7. Security

Licences are digitally signed, and access to our licence service is limited. Because your records live on your own computer, keeping that computer secure (passwords, updates, backups) is also important.

8. Children

Atlas is a business tool and is not meant for children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Atlas develops. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
